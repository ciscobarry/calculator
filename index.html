Understanding the AFT Scoring System

The AFT scoring system is structured to measure different aspects of physical fitness. It typically includes components such as aerobic capacity, muscular strength, and body composition. Each section contributes a specific number of points, which are then combined to produce a final score. The total score determines whether an individual meets the required fitness standards or needs improvement.

Aerobic fitness is usually assessed through timed runs or alternative cardio exercises. Strength is measured through push-ups and sit-ups, while body composition evaluates weight relative to height. Each category has its own scoring criteria, making it important for individuals to understand how their performance translates into points.
